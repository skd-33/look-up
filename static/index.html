About Look Up

Look Up runs entirely on your machine. The planet positions come from a local copy of NASA's DE421 ephemeris, computed by Skyfield. No cloud service, no tracking, no telemetry. Your location stays in your config file and never leaves your computer.

The short nudge at the top is written by a small language model running on your own hardware through Ollama. If the model is offline, a plain sentence takes its place. Either way the facts are always computed fresh from the ephemeris, never cached or invented.

This project exists because stargazing is one of the few things that gets better when you put your phone down. The timer is there to help you do that. Step outside, look up, and give your eyes a few minutes to adjust.
